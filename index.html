The question every YouTube review answers. But a shoe that gets 9/10 for a power forward might be 4/10 for a shifty guard. One review, two completely different athletes — zero useful information.

You buy the hype. The shoes arrive. They look great. But something feels off — too heavy, no traction on your cuts, blisters in the wrong places. Another $150 wasted on someone else's shoe.

Reviews built for one player type

WearTesters, Tommy Liu, Sole Brothers — great content. But they review shoes from their perspective. If your movement patterns don't match theirs, their recommendation is useless for you.

Because you're not everyone. A speed guard needs different traction than a paint anchor. An older hooper needs different cushioning than an 18-year-old. Same shoe, completely different results.

A massive portion of basketball players have wider feet than standard shoe lasts are built for — and nobody addresses this directly in their recommendations. We do.

Outdoor players buying indoor shoes

Indoor basketball shoes dissolve on concrete in weeks. Outdoor players need different materials, different rubber compounds, and different construction. Most reviews never mention the surface you actually play on.
