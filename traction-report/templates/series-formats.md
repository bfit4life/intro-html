# Content Series Formats — Traction Report

## 1. Sturdy | Shaky | Not It (Flagship)
Verdict reviews. Full performance breakdown. The franchise.
- YouTube: 8–15 min full review
- Shorts: The verdict moment
- IG: Performance-focused cut
- TikTok: Hot take reactive
- Threads: Opinion drop

## 2. Traction Lab
Outsole technology breakdown. Technical. Specific. For hoopers who want to know WHY.
- Focus: sole geometry, rubber compound, pivot points, herringbone pattern
- Tone: More technical than other series but still Bryan's voice

## 3. On The Feet
Lifestyle + styling. Second priority. Visual-heavy.
- Best for IG carousels and TikTok aesthetic content

## 4. The Drop Report
Release news + takes. Time-sensitive. Publish fast.
- Threads-first. Then TikTok. YouTube if the drop is big enough.

## 5. Movement Check
Performance test clips. On-court. Real conditions.
- Short-form first. Raw footage energy. No over-production.

---

## Content Hierarchy (Non-Negotiable)
TIER 1: YouTube Long-Form → source of truth
TIER 2: YouTube Shorts + Instagram Reels (cut from long-form)
TIER 3: TikTok + Threads (derived or reactive)

Rule: Write one long-form with the Shorts moment already identified. Everything else derives.
