## EMAIL 3: How We Test Shoes (The Pipeline Explained)

**Subject line:** The test most reviews never run.

**Preview text:** Fresh out the box traction is the easy test. Here's the one that actually matters.

---

Hey,

Here's a test I want you to try.

Find a pair of basketball shoes you've been playing in. Take them off after a long run. Press your thumb firmly into the insole foam. Hold it for a second. Release.

How fast does the foam bounce back?

If it snaps back in two seconds — the foam still has life. If your thumbprint sits there for five or more seconds — the cushion is compressed. Your joints absorbed what the foam was supposed to. You've been hooping in a shoe that stopped protecting you a while ago.

That's the thumb test. It's one of six tests in the TTR Performance Matrix. And it's the kind of test that almost never appears in a standard sneaker review.

Here's the full matrix:

**1. Movement Efficiency** — Does the sole flex match your natural foot movement? Does the shoe fight you or move with you?

**2. Traction + Grip** — Not just on clean hardwood. We test dusty floors, outdoor surfaces, and traction performance after 60 minutes of real play. Traction that cracks under fatigue is not traction at all.

**3. Containment Under Pressure** — Does the shoe hold your foot on a hard lateral cut? We slow down the footage and look for foot migration inside the shoe. You can't feel it happening in real time, but you feel it in your ankle later.

**4. Performance Sustainability** — Does the shoe perform the same at minute 60 as it did at minute 10? We document exactly when and where performance changes. Most shoes have a drop-off point. We find it.

**5. Recovery + Joint Protection** — What does your body feel after a game in this shoe? The foam test. The ankle fatigue accumulation. The post-session feedback that tells you whether this shoe is extending your game or slowly ending it.

**6. Trust Factor** — After all the data, would I wear this shoe in a game I cared about? Would I recommend it to a player I respect? This is the gut-level synthesis of everything else.

At the end of every test, one verdict: STURDY. SHAKY. NOT IT.

That's the system. That's why the verdicts mean something.
