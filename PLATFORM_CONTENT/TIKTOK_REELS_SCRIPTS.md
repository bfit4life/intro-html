## SCRIPT 1: FIRST LOOK
### Shoe: Nike LeBron 22

---

**HOOK (0-2 seconds)**

[Bryan holds shoe directly at camera, one sharp shake]

SPOKEN: "First impression? This shoe has something to prove."

ON-SCREEN TEXT: "FIRST LOOK: LEBRON 22 🔬"

---

**BODY (2-25 seconds)**

[Cut to Bryan inspecting upper, fingers pressing on material]

SPOKEN: "Upper's stiff. More structure than last year's model. That's a good sign for wide footers — the heel cup is deep."

[Flip shoe, show sole]

SPOKEN: "Traction pattern — full herringbone. No zones, no gimmicks. We respect that. This should bite on clean hardwood."

[Bryan laces shoe up, pulls tight]

SPOKEN: "Lacing system's got real tension. Midfoot feels locked. The cushion stack — thick. We're talking Heavy Forward energy on this build."

[Stand, take one hard step]

SPOKEN: "But here's what we don't know yet — does it hold at minute 60? Does the containment break down laterally?"

---

**ENDING / CTA (25-35 seconds)**

[Direct to camera, serious]

SPOKEN: "That's what the Traction Test is for. Thursday. We'll have answers."

ON-SCREEN TEXT: "TRACTION TEST — THURSDAY"
CAPTION TEXT: "FOLLOW so you don't miss the verdict. 🏀"

---

**VISUAL DIRECTION NOTES:**
- Film in good natural light or ring light, not a dark gym
- Background: clean gym wall OR a simple lab-aesthetic setup (white wall, black shelf)
- No jump cuts — hold each shot 2-3 seconds before cutting
- Show shoe CLOSE. Tread, upper texture, heel cup. Make it feel like a real inspection.
- Bryan's energy: focused, not hype. Like a scout evaluating a prospect, not a hype man at a drop.

## SCRIPT 2: TRACTION TEST
### Shoe: Adidas Harden Vol. 8

---

**HOOK (0-2 seconds)**

[Bryan mid-cut, freeze frame, text drops in]

ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Does it GRIP or does it SLIP?"

[Unfreeze — Bryan completes the cut, looks at camera]

SPOKEN: "We found out."

---

**BODY (2-40 seconds)**

[Cut to court. Bryan in shoes, walking into frame]

SPOKEN: "Adidas Harden Vol. 8. Traction Test. Let's go."

[Series of hard cuts — lateral shuffle left, lateral shuffle right, hard stop, 45-degree cut]

SPOKEN (voiceover): "Pattern is a modified herringbone with flex channels. On clean hardwood — grips immediately. No warm-up period. That's actually rare."

[Slow-mo of sole on floor mid-cut]

SPOKEN: "Watch the forefoot contact. Full bite. No slide."

[Bryan takes a hard stop, almost stumbles]

SPOKEN: "Now here's where it gets interesting. Hard stop off the right foot — the heel caught a little. Not dangerous. But a Shifty Guard who relies on split-second stops is going to feel that."

[Wipes sole with towel, repeats hard stop]

SPOKEN: "Dusty court? Different story. This tread clogs. We tested it outdoors — I don't recommend it. The rubber compound is too soft for concrete."

[Cut to direct camera address]

SPOKEN: "For the indoor hooper? Solid grip. For the outdoor run? Look elsewhere."

---

**ENDING / VERDICT DROP (40-55 seconds)**

[Bryan holds shoe at chest, camera tight on face]

SPOKEN: "Traction verdict on the Harden Vol. 8:"

[Hard cut to graphic — white text on black background]

ON-SCREEN TEXT: "TRACTION: STURDY (INDOORS)"
ON-SCREEN TEXT: "TRACTION: SHAKY (OUTDOORS)"

SPOKEN: "Know your court. Know your shoe."

---

**VISUAL DIRECTION NOTES:**
- This script needs actual court footage. Well-lit gym is essential.
- Slow-mo clips of sole contact are key — shoot at 60fps minimum and slow to 30%
- Sweat is authentic. Don't wipe your face before filming.
- The freeze frame hook requires editing — plan this in post
- Camera can be on a tripod for wide shot OR a friend for tracking
- Verdict graphic: clean, black background, white CAPS text. No color gradients. Lab aesthetic.

## SCRIPT 3: CONTAINMENT CHECK
### Shoe: New Balance 1000v1

---

**HOOK (0-2 seconds)**

[Bryan plants hard left, shoe nearly rolls, catches himself]

SPOKEN: "THAT. Right there. That's the test."

ON-SCREEN TEXT: "CONTAINMENT CHECK ⚠️"

---

**BODY (2-35 seconds)**

[Slow it down — Bryan at camera, breathed out]

SPOKEN: "New Balance 1000. Containment Check. And I want to talk about the ankle collar specifically."

[Close-up on ankle area of shoe]

SPOKEN: "Low cut. Which means — your ankle is on its own. The shoe cannot save you. So the question is: does the upper hold your foot *inside* the shoe when you cut hard?"

[Back to court — series of hard lateral cuts]

SPOKEN (voiceover): "Narrow footers — this shoe is a problem. The toebox has extra room. On a hard lateral cut, your foot is shifting inside the shoe before the sole even responds."

[Slow-mo of foot shifting inside shoe during cut — show it clearly]

SPOKEN: "See that? That's foot migration. You cut right, but your foot doesn't actually lock until a half-second later. That half-second costs you a step. Or costs you an ankle."

[Direct camera address]

SPOKEN: "Wide footer? Different story. The extra space fits you. Foot sits flat. Containment's actually solid."

---

**ENDING / VERDICT (35-48 seconds)**

SPOKEN: "New Balance 1000. Containment verdict:"

ON-SCREEN TEXT: "WIDE FOOTER → STURDY"
ON-SCREEN TEXT: "NARROW FOOTER → NOT IT"

SPOKEN: "Archetype matters. Don't buy the hype. Buy the fit."

---

**VISUAL DIRECTION NOTES:**
- The foot migration shot is the key visual. You may need a second camera low to the ground to capture this. Worth the setup.
- Slow-mo is essential — this content depends on visible detail
- Bryan's tone here is slightly more clinical than usual. This is the "lab" voice — still punchy, but precise.
- The verdict split (wide vs. narrow) is a key differentiator — make sure on-screen text lands clearly and sequentially

## SCRIPT 4: RECOVERY CHECK
### Shoe: Under Armour Curry 12

---

**HOOK (0-2 seconds)**

[Bryan, visibly sweated out, holds shoe up]

SPOKEN: "I just played 90 minutes in these. Here's what my feet are telling me."

ON-SCREEN TEXT: "RECOVERY CHECK — MINUTE 90 🔬"

---

**BODY (2-50 seconds)**

[Bryan sits on bench, removes shoe — close camera on foot]

SPOKEN (voiceover): "Recovery Check. This is where we find out if the cushion held. If the foam bounced back. If your foot is going to thank you or curse you tomorrow."

[Cut to pre-game clip of fresh shoe, then cut back to post-game]

SPOKEN: "Started the session with full stack response. The Curry 12's Warp cushion felt live — real energy return off the forefoot."

[Bryan presses thumb into insole of removed shoe]

SPOKEN: "After 90 minutes? Thumb test. Press the foam. How much compression is left?"

[Shows clear compression in insole]

SPOKEN: "It's compressing — but bouncing back. That's a good sign. Foam isn't dead."

[Bryan rotates ankle, grimaces slightly]

SPOKEN: "But here's the real Recovery Check data. My left ankle — the one I cut on harder — is feeling it. Not pain. Just fatigue. The lateral support doesn't reinforce the ankle under repeated load."

[Direct camera]

SPOKEN: "For the Explosive Wing who's doing one game? Great shoe. For the Older Hooper doing game two of a double-header? Your joints need more protection than this foam provides."

---

**ENDING (50-65 seconds)**

SPOKEN: "Recovery verdict on the Curry 12:"

ON-SCREEN TEXT: "ONE-GAME HOOPER → STURDY"
ON-SCREEN TEXT: "HIGH-VOLUME HOOPER → SHAKY"

SPOKEN: "Great shoe. But your body has more games to play. Protect accordingly."

ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for the full Recovery Check series."

---

**VISUAL DIRECTION NOTES:**
- This is the most voiceover-heavy pillar. Bryan doesn't need to be in every shot — cut to close-ups of the shoe, the foam, the foot.
- The pre/post comparison is a powerful visual — plan to film a quick clip at the START of the session for this purpose
- Bryan being visibly fatigued is authentic and important. Don't clean up before filming this.
- The thumb-press insole test is a signature TTR visual — make it a recurring motif in every Recovery Check
- Slower pacing than Traction Test. Let the analysis breathe.

## SCRIPT 5: STILL HOOPABLE IN 2026?
### Shoe: Nike Kobe 8 Protro

---

**HOOK (0-2 seconds)**

[Bryan holds up Kobe 8s — crispy white/gold colorway — directly at lens]

SPOKEN: "2013 called. And honestly? These still might win."

ON-SCREEN TEXT: "STILL HOOPABLE IN 2026? 🐍"

---

**BODY (2-50 seconds)**

[Cut to Bryan lacing up Kobes on a bench]

SPOKEN: "The Kobe 8 Protro. People love these for the culture. But we're not here for the culture. We're here to answer one question — is this shoe still a real basketball shoe in 2026?"

[Court footage — Bryan in Kobes, quick cuts, jab steps, lateral movement]

SPOKEN (voiceover): "Traction first. Original herringbone pattern. Still grips. Hardwood — no issues. The rubber compound held up. Protro's outsole is new production, so you're not playing in a 12-year-old sole."

[Close-up of shoe during cut]

SPOKEN: "Containment — this is where it's interesting. Low cut. Mamba build. Zero ankle support by design. Kobe wanted to feel the floor. If you're a Shifty Guard or Explosive Wing — that's your archetype. This shoe was built for you."

[Bryan stops, direct address]

SPOKEN: "But if you're a Heavy Forward? Running in these? You're gambling. Your joints need more stack. This foam — Phylon — is not the recovery story a big man needs."

[Quick cut montage: Bryan cutting hard, planting, stopping]

SPOKEN: "Speed? Still elite. Court feel? Some of the best. Cushion depth? Limited."

---

**ENDING / VERDICT (50-65 seconds)**

ON-SCREEN TEXT: "STILL HOOPABLE?"

SPOKEN: "Yes. But only for the right archetype."

ON-SCREEN TEXT: "SHIFTY GUARD / EXPLOSIVE WING → STURDY"
ON-SCREEN TEXT: "HEAVY FORWARD / OLDER HOOPER → NOT IT"

SPOKEN: "Know what you are. Then decide if the Kobe 8 is your shoe. We gave you the data. The move is yours."

ON-SCREEN TEXT: "What's your verdict? Drop it below. 👇"

---

**VISUAL DIRECTION NOTES:**
- The nostalgic colorway is visually powerful — film it. But don't let the aesthetic overshadow the performance analysis.
- Shoot some footage outside or in a slightly gritty gym to contrast with the clean-shoe look — the visual tension is interesting
- Comments will be emotional on Kobe content. Respect the legacy explicitly in the script. Bryan does this naturally, but make sure it's in the tone.
- The verdict split here is a major engagement driver. Pin a comment asking: "Which archetype are you?"

## SCRIPT 6: COMMUNITY / CULTURE
### Theme: Archetype Reveal — "Which Hooper Are You?"

---

**HOOK (0-2 seconds)**

[Bryan at camera, straight-faced, no setup]

SPOKEN: "Every hooper thinks they're a Shifty Guard. Most of them aren't."

ON-SCREEN TEXT: "WHAT'S YOUR ARCHETYPE? 🧪"

---

**BODY (2-30 seconds)**

[Fast-cut series — each archetype shown in 2-second clips with text overlay]

SPOKEN: "We break hoopers into seven movement profiles. Because the right shoe for one player is the wrong shoe for another. That's not an opinion. That's physics."

[CLIP: Fast dribble-drive, crossover, pull-up] ON-SCREEN TEXT: "SHIFTY GUARD — quick twitch, needs grip and court feel"

[CLIP: Explosive first step, dunk attempt] ON-SCREEN TEXT: "EXPLOSIVE WING — vertical load, needs cushion and containment"

[CLIP: Post move, physical body positioning] ON-SCREEN TEXT: "HEAVY FORWARD — joint protection, needs wide base and stack"

[CLIP: Slow-but-smart veteran hooper] ON-SCREEN TEXT: "OLDER HOOPER — recovery priority, knee and ankle support"

[CLIP: Jump, block attempt, quick lateral] ON-SCREEN TEXT: "VOLLEYBALL-STYLE MOVER — multi-direction, needs lateral plate"

[CLIP: Wide-foot hooper, shoe slightly bowing] ON-SCREEN TEXT: "WIDE FOOTER — fit first, toebox volume critical"

[CLIP: Outdoor court, rougher play] ON-SCREEN TEXT: "OUTDOOR HOOPER — durability over everything"

---

**ENDING (30-45 seconds)**

[Bryan, direct camera]

SPOKEN: "Which one are you? Drop it in the comments. And if you don't know yet — we've got a full archetype guide on the channel."

ON-SCREEN TEXT: "COMMENT YOUR ARCHETYPE 👇"
ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Link in bio → Free Guide"

SPOKEN: "Know your game. Know your shoe."

---

**VISUAL DIRECTION NOTES:**
- This script is more of a produced piece — you need clips of different players/playing styles. Use real pickup game footage if possible.
- Alternatively, Bryan can play each archetype himself — quick wardrobe/shoe change between clips
- On-screen text for each archetype should pop in fast and clean — no fancy animations, just bold white text on semi-transparent background
- Comment engagement is the entire goal of this piece. Pin a stickied comment asking the archetype question before it even starts getting replies.
- End with a like/follow CTA — this is a community piece, growth-oriented
