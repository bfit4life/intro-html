## The Traction Report Fit-Testing Protocol

1. **Bring your real socks.**
2. **Walk, don't just stand.**
3. **Lateral shuffle test** — 5 slides each direction; watch for foot shift inside a too-narrow shoe.
4. **Thumb-width toe test** — but also check side-to-side room, not just length.
5. **Heel-lock test.**
6. **Simulate your actual movement.**
7. **Give it 10 minutes** before deciding.
8. **Always ask for wide-width (2E/4E) availability before sizing up** — a half-size up doesn't fix a width problem, it just moves it.
