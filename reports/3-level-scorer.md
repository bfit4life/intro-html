### The Mechanical Breakdown — Why Your Shoe Requirements Are Different

High-volume shooters load their lower body the same way on every rep — catch, gather, rise, release — hundreds of times a game across practice and play. That repetition demands balanced cushioning that won't go dead over volume, plus a secure midfoot and heel lockdown so your base never shifts mid-shot. You also need real lateral support, because most of your misses-to-makes swing on how well you handle a closeout before you ever get to shoot.

Your priority order should be: **secure lockdown first, balanced cushioning second, lateral support third, consistent traction fourth.**

## The Traction Report Fit-Testing Protocol

1. **Bring your real socks.**
2. **Walk, don't just stand.**
3. **Lateral shuffle test** — 5 slides each direction, focused on closeout footwork.
4. **Thumb-width toe test.**
5. **Heel-lock test.**
6. **Simulate your actual movement** — catch, gather, and rise into a shot right in the store.
7. **Give it 10 minutes** before deciding.
8. **Go true to size first**, then adjust.
