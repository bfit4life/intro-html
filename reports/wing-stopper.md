### The Mechanical Breakdown — Why Your Shoe Requirements Are Different

Lateral defensive slides put different stress on a shoe than forward movement does — your foot is loading and unloading side-to-side repeatedly, and soft cushioning that feels great running forward will compress unevenly and feel mushy on a slide. You need a stiffer, more responsive cushioning setup specifically tuned for side-to-side stability, plus the deepest traction pattern you can find.

Your priority order should be: **multi-directional traction first (non-negotiable), heel lockdown second, lateral stability third, weight fourth.**

## The Traction Report Fit-Testing Protocol

1. **Bring your real socks.**
2. **Walk, don't just stand.**
3. **Lateral shuffle test** — this one matters most for you. Do 8-10 slides each direction, not just 5.
4. **Thumb-width toe test.**
5. **Heel-lock test.**
6. **Simulate your actual movement** — closeout sprint, plant, slide, contest.
7. **Give it 10 minutes** before deciding.
8. **Go true to size first**, then adjust.
