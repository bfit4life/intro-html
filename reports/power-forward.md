### The Mechanical Breakdown — Why Your Shoe Requirements Are Different

Landing from a rebound or a roll-and-finish puts far more vertical force through your foot than a guard's cut ever will, and you're doing it while absorbing contact from defenders at the same time. A shoe without real impact cushioning in the heel will leave that force to your joints instead. You also need a wider, more stable base than perimeter players — your game involves fighting for positioning with your feet planted, not just sprinting past someone.

Your priority order should be: **impact cushioning first, ankle support second, durability third, traction fourth.**

## The Traction Report Fit-Testing Protocol

1. **Bring your real socks.**
2. **Walk, don't just stand.**
3. **Lateral shuffle test** — 5 slides each direction.
4. **Thumb-width toe test.**
5. **Heel-lock test.**
6. **Simulate your actual movement** — set a screen, roll, and land a jump stop right in the store.
7. **Give it 10 minutes** before deciding.
8. **Go true to size first**, then adjust — consider a half-size up if you wear thicker socks for impact protection.
