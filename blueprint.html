Executive Summary
What Traction Report is and why it works

What It Is

Traction Report is a basketball footwear matching platform built around a proprietary player archetype system. Where traditional sneaker review channels answer "Is this shoe good?", Traction Report answers "Is this shoe good for you?" — because every player moves differently, and a shoe that earns a 9/10 for a Shifty Guard may earn a 4/10 for a Paint Anchor.

Why It Works

  • The sneaker review market is crowded at the product level. Personalization is wide open.
  • People pay for identity, not information. Myers-Briggs, StrengthsFinder, and Enneagram generate $2B+ annually by telling people who they are. Traction Report does the same for basketball players.
  • The $6B basketball shoe market sells to athletes who overwhelmingly don't know which shoe actually fits their game — they just follow what's popular.
  • There is zero brand with a personality-based, movement-profile-based footwear matching system. The positioning is completely open.

Why Athletes Care

  • Every basketball player has asked "what shoe should I buy?" at least once.
  • Parents spending $150 on shoes for their kid want validation they're buying the right thing.
  • Older hoopers with joint pain want specific cushioning guidance, not generic reviews.
  • Players with wide feet are actively frustrated by lack of fit-specific guidance.
  • The identity component — "I'm a Shifty Guard" — creates tribal belonging and shareability.

06
Email Sequence
5-email drip that builds trust and drives purchases

Email 1 — Sent Immediately After Assessment

Subject: "You're a [Archetype Name]. Here's what that means."

  • Confirm their archetype and score
  • Explain their movement profile in plain language
  • Give one immediate, actionable tip: "Next time you're in a shoe store, here's one thing to check that most people skip…"
  • CTA: "Reply to this email with your current shoe — I'll tell you if it fits your archetype."

Email 2 — Day 2

Subject: "The shoe everyone recommends — and why [Archetype]s should avoid it"

  • Name a popular, well-reviewed shoe (e.g., Nike Kobe Protro for someone who isn't a guard)
  • Explain exactly why the movement profile mismatch creates problems
  • Reinforce the archetype identity: "As a [Archetype], your priority is X, not Y"
  • Soft CTA: Their top recommendation at mid-range with affiliate link

Email 3 — Day 4

Subject: "What [NBA Player] and you have in common"

  • Name an NBA player who shares their archetype (De'Aaron Fox for Speed Guard, Kyrie for Shifty Guard, etc.)
  • Break down what shoes that player wears and why
  • Reinforce the premium recommendation with context
  • CTA: $29 Shoe Match Report offer ("I built a deeper version of this analysis just for your archetype — 7 pages, $29")

Email 4 — Day 7

Subject: "The $80 shoe that outperforms every $150 option for [Archetype]s"

  • Reveal the best budget pick with a detailed performance breakdown
  • Show the price-to-performance argument specific to their archetype
  • CTA: Direct affiliate link. Also mention Video Consultation for players who want personalized feedback on their specific game.

Email 5 — Day 14

Subject: "Has your archetype changed? (Retake when this happens)"

  • Explain that archetypes evolve with your game (gaining muscle → Power Forward traits, aging → Older Hooper modifier)
  • Invite them to share their archetype score on social media with a template
  • CTA: Membership soft pitch ("I'm building a monthly community for hoopers who take their footwear seriously — interested?") + gauge demand before building

13
Investor Pitch
Why Traction Report can scale to a significant business

The Problem

Basketball players spend $6B+ annually on footwear based almost entirely on social media hype, brand loyalty, and peer recommendations — not on their individual movement profiles. The result: players consistently buy the wrong shoes for how they actually play, wasting money and in some cases contributing to preventable injury.

The Solution

Traction Report is the CarFax for basketball shoes — a proprietary archetype-based footwear matching system that assigns players a movement profile and delivers personalized shoe recommendations. Our 10-archetype system (7 primary + 3 modifiers) creates 28 distinct player profiles, each with unique footwear needs that no generic review can address.

The Market

  • TAM: $6B+ global basketball footwear market, 26M basketball players in the US
  • SAM: ~8M engaged basketball players who actively research shoe purchases
  • SOM: 500K players who complete personalized assessments in Year 1

Why It Scales

  • The assessment creates a proprietary dataset that improves recommendations over time
  • The archetype identity creates organic sharing and virality (Myers-Briggs effect)
  • Multiple revenue streams (affiliate, reports, consultations, membership, SaaS, sponsorship)
  • B2B potential: shoe brands pay for aggregate archetype data to inform product development
  • Expandable beyond basketball: soccer, tennis, running — same archetype methodology, new markets
