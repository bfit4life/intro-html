# STILL HOOPABLE IN 2026? — EPISODE 1
## Shoe: Nike Kobe 4 Protro

**Series:** Still Hoopable in 2026?
**Episode:** 001
**Target Length:** 60–90 seconds
**Format:** Viral format. Debate-starting. Real performance evaluation wrapped in nostalgia tension.

**Why this shoe:**
The Kobe 4 is not just a basketball shoe. It's a cultural artifact. When it dropped in 2008, it changed the conversation about what a basketball shoe could be — low-cut at a time when every player, coach, and trainer in the country said ankle support required high-tops. Kobe proved them wrong. Or did he? The Protro brings back the same silhouette with modern materials. But "Still Hoopable" asks the real question: does the performance hold up against what's on the market in 2026? And the controversial answer is — it depends who's asking.

## FULL PRODUCTION SCRIPT

---

**[HOOK — Immediate debate. Drop the bold claim before they settle in.]*

*[Bryan, direct to camera. No warmup.]*

**Bryan:**
"The Kobe 4 is one of the most important basketball shoes ever made.

And in 2026, for most of the people buying it —
it's a collector's shoe pretending to be a performance shoe.

Let's get into it."

---

**[HISTORICAL CONTEXT — Brief, efficient, earned.]*

**Bryan (V.O.):**
*[B-roll style footage — archival court shots, Kobe 24 era footage, 2008 aesthetics. Handled with respect.]*

"In 2008, the Kobe 4 broke the rule that every coach had preached for 30 years:
wear high-tops. Protect your ankles. Don't risk it.

Kobe wore low-cuts to an NBA Finals.
He won.
The rule died.

The shoe was ahead of its time in 2008.
The question is whether it's still ahead in 2026."

---

**[MODERN PERFORMANCE TEST — The lab results.]*

*[Cut to court. Bryan testing the Kobe 4 Protro — real movement, not staged.]*

**Bryan (V.O.):**
"We ran the Kobe 4 Protro through the full TTR lab.
Here's what the numbers say.

Traction:
The Protro uses an updated outsole pattern from the original.
On a clean court — solid. Not elite by 2026 standards, but solid.
On a dusty court — average. The original herringbone held up better than some modern takes.
Traction verdict: competitive. Not the best on the market today, but not embarrassing.

Cushion:
This is where the era shows.
The Kobe 4 Protro uses Zoom Air — forefoot and heel.
In 2008, this was cutting-edge. In 2026, it's adequate.
React, Zoom Strobel, Air Cushion systems have passed it.
If you're comparing this to what's protecting your joints in a Sabrina 3 or an OG-1 —
the Kobe 4 Protro is playing from behind.

Containment:
Low-cut by design. Always was.
If you have ankle concerns — this shoe is not going to reassure you.
If you're a guard with strong ankles and solid proprioception — you're fine.
But Kobe had elite body mechanics and trained his ankle stability specifically.
Most of us are not Kobe."

---

**[THE VERDICT — Nostalgia vs. performance reality.]*

*[Bryan, direct camera. No hesitation.]*

**Bryan:**
"Does nostalgia match performance reality?

Mostly no.

The Kobe 4 Protro is a great shoe for what it is.
It is a well-made tribute to a legendary silhouette.
The craftsmanship on the Protro is real.
The materials are cleaner than the OG.

But the performance gap between the Kobe 4 Protro
and what's being built for basketball in 2026
is real.
And it costs your body more than you think —
especially if you're playing multiple sessions a week."

**Bryan:**
"Still Hoopable verdict:

**YES — for the right player.**

Specifically:
If you're a Floor General or a Slasher
under 200 pounds,
with strong ankles,
playing on clean courts,
two days a week or less —
the Kobe 4 Protro can still work.

If you're any other archetype —
Older Hooper, Heavy Guard, Stretch Big —
this shoe is going to feel like what it is.
A great shoe from 2008.

Wear it because it means something.
Just know what you're wearing it into."

---

**[THE CONTROVERSIAL TAKE — One strong opinion. Comment section igniter.]*

**Bryan:**
"And here's the take that's going to make some of you angry:

Kobe switched to a different shoe at the end of his career for a reason.

The same player who convinced the world that low-cuts were the future
spent his final seasons in shoes with more support.
More cushion. More protection.

He wasn't wrong about the Kobe 4 in 2008.
He also wasn't wrong when he changed his mind.

The Kobe 4 was built for a specific version of Kobe Bryant.
Not the player who just laced up.
Not most of us.

That's not a knock on the shoe.
That's the truth about every shoe —
and it's exactly why The Traction Report exists."

---

**[CLOSE — TTR brand stamp.]*

**Bryan (V.O.):**
"Still Hoopable in 2026 — the format that asks what nobody else does.
The Traction Report. We don't review sneakers. We lab them."
