# TRACTION TEST — EPISODE 1
## Shoe: Nike Ja 3

**Series:** Traction Test
**Episode:** 001
**Target Length:** 45–60 seconds
**Format:** Authority content. Technical. Movement-focused. Data-driven.

**Why this shoe for launch:**
Ja Morant's game is traction. Explosive cuts, unpredictable direction changes, getting into the paint at full speed and stopping on a dime. The Ja 3 carries that identity. If any shoe in the market should have elite traction, it's this one. That expectation creates perfect tension — does it deliver? That's the test. And this shoe has a controversial answer depending on court conditions.

---

## FULL PRODUCTION SCRIPT

---

**[HOOK — First 2 seconds. Bold traction claim. Drop it hard.]*

*[Shot: Bryan's feet on hardwood. Hard cut. Shoes squeak. Freeze frame. Text hits.]*

**Bryan (V.O.):**
"Nike built this shoe around one of the most explosive players alive.

So the traction better be elite.

Let's find out."

---

**[TESTING SEQUENCE — Hard stops, lateral cuts, dust performance.]*

*[Bryan in motion on court. Real game conditions. Not staged drills.]*

**Bryan (V.O.):**
"Hard stop test first.
Full speed down the lane. Plant. Stop.

That's the bite. That's what you need at top speed.
The herringbone on the Ja 3 is aggressive — it's not a subtle pattern.
And on a clean floor? It grips.

Second test: lateral cut.
Change of direction. Side to side.
Your foot needs to trust the shoe. Or you're not going full speed.

On a clean floor, this shoe passes. No hesitation.
But basketball floors are not always clean.

That brings us to the dust test.
Because this is where Ja 3 gets complicated."

---

**[DUST TEST MOMENT — The critical performance point.]*

*[Shot: Court with visible dust patches — under the basket, near the sideline. Bryan runs through them deliberately.]*

**Bryan (V.O.):**
"Dust. Under the basket. Baseline cuts. Transition.
You know the spots.

On a dusty court — the Ja 3 slips.
Not catastrophically. But enough that you feel it.
Enough that you hesitate.
And hesitation in basketball costs you.

The rubber compound is soft, which is why it grips so well clean.
But that same softness picks up dust fast.
You're wiping your soles every two possessions if you're in a gym with any kind of foot traffic.

That is a real-world problem for a real-world player."

---

**[VERDICT REVEAL — Controlled. Direct. Archetype-specific.]*

*[Cut to Bryan, direct to camera. Settled. Not heated — just honest.]*

**Bryan:**
"Here's the Traction Report verdict on the Ja 3.

On a clean floor: STURDY.
On a dusty floor: SHAKY.

That matters.
If you play in a controlled facility, a clean gym, hardwood that gets mopped —
you're good. This shoe is built to move.

But if you play pickup. Open gym. School gym.
The kind of courts most of us actually play on —
you need to know what you're getting into.

Archetype call: this is a Floor General shoe. A Slasher shoe.
Light, low, fast.
But it is not a dusty-court specialist.

STURDY in the right conditions.
SHAKY in the wrong ones.

That's the Traction Report call on the Ja 3.
We don't guess. We test."
